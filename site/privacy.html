bareflow

privacy

  • bareflow logs neither the address you convert nor you. Cloudflare carries every request, the address included. The site you name gets the request from Cloudflare, not from your IP, with a Cloudflare header naming the domain it came through.
  • A pasted page never leaves your browser: no Turnstile, no fetch.
  • Converting an address loads Cloudflare Turnstile, which reads signals such as your IP and browser to tell a person from a bot. bareflow sends its token and your IP to Cloudflare to check it. Turnstile privacy addendum
  • Cloudflare hosts this site and sees each request to it. Cloudflare privacy policy
  • The conversion runs in your browser, which keeps two settings: the theme and more or less. bareflow sets no cookies.
  • "report wrong output" opens a GitHub issue with the address filled in. Nothing is sent until you submit it.
